# Release Notes

Version 1.0.0 is the final graduation-project research release. The primary
benchmark completed 1,350 unique episodes with no missing or unresolved failed
runs; six ablations completed 180 episodes. The compiled paper reports both
improvements and negative results.

This is a simulation research artifact, not certified safety software. Learned
policies did not solve full grid missions, and deployment on a real
construction site is unsupported.
